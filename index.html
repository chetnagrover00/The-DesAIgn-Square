Why we started this

Five years into design rooms and community spaces, one thing kept nagging me: we're brilliant at learning together — and terrible at actually connecting.

Every event ended the same way — a phone full of new LinkedIn requests, and not one person I'd text on Monday. The ideas were everywhere; the friendships weren't. So I started The डिज़AIन Square: small rooms where you make something with your hands, have real gupshup, and walk out with people — not just notes. One small square at a time.

FAQ

Your questions, answered.

Is it free to attend?

No — this is a paid event, and that's on purpose. Your ticket covers everything: activity materials, food, and the full experience — put together so you get the best. Nothing extra to buy on the day.

Can I come alone?

Yes — and most people do. Every Square opens with an easy icebreaker and stays small, so you're never left standing in a corner. You'll be chatting with people within minutes.

What actually happens at a Square?

A mix of hands-on making, a little design and AI, good food, and real conversation. You'll build something, meet interesting people, and leave with genuine connections.

How do the limited seats work?

We keep each Square small — around 20–30 people — so it stays personal. Registrations happen on Commudle, where we manage seats and tickets. Join the WhatsApp community to get the heads-up the moment they go live.
